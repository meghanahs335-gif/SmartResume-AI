# 🚀 SmartResume AI

An AI-powered resume builder designed to help students and fresh graduates create polished, recruiter-ready resumes quickly and easily.

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Interactive Resume Builder** — Add and edit personal and professional information through guided sections.
- 🤖 **AI Suggestions** — Get AI-assisted suggestions to improve resume content.
- 📊 **Resume Strength Score** — Quickly evaluate the overall strength and completeness of your resume.
- 👀 **Live Resume Preview** — See your formatted resume update while editing.
- 📄 **PDF Export** — Download the completed resume as a PDF.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Designed to work across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- 🎨 **Clean, Professional UI** — Simple interface focused on usability and readability.

## 🤖 AI Integration

SmartResume AI includes an AI-powered resume improvement workflow that provides suggestions for making resume content clearer and more impactful.

The project was developed with the assistance of AI-powered development tools for frontend ideation, implementation, debugging, and UI refinement.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students and fresh graduates often struggle to create professional resumes because they are unsure about formatting, content structure, and how to present their skills and experiences effectively.

SmartResume AI brings resume creation, content improvement, feedback, live preview, and PDF export into one simple application.

## 💡 Solution

SmartResume AI provides a guided resume-building experience where users can enter their information, improve their content, view the result instantly, check their resume strength, and download the final resume as a PDF.
